Product Description:

Innovative Digestive Biscuits are a premium, fiber-rich snack crafted to offer a wholesome and satisfying balance of health and taste. Made with high-quality whole wheat flour and natural wheat bran, these biscuits deliver a distinctively hearty crunch and a rich, grainy texture. At approximately 77 kcal per biscuit, they provide a guilt-free energy boost, making them the perfect, lightly sweet companion for your daily tea or coffee, or a versatile crust ingredient for your favorite desserts.
